## Blocking checks before the shooting board
- Target commit inspected, not assumed from an older note.
- Camera owner identified. If one exists, the plan sends intents to it and adds no second controller.
- Every media item has a source record and rights status. Unknown rights are reference only.
- Each system keeps its own identity. No integration between systems is implied unless inspected.
